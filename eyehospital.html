Rohini Devi Eye Hospital

Our hospital provides free and affordable eye care services to those in need. We perform cataract surgeries, vision screenings, and community outreach programs to prevent blindness.

Our dedicated doctors and volunteers ensure that every patient receives compassionate and high-quality treatment.
