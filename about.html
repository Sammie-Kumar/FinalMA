About Us

The Rohini Devi Rotary was founded to uplift communities through service, education, and sustainable development. Over the years, we have grown into a vibrant organization dedicated to meaningful change.

Our values—compassion, collaboration, and innovation—guide every initiative, from healthcare programs to environmental stewardship.
