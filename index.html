Welcome to the Rohini Devi Rotary

Col. Binay Kumar (Retd) and Mrs. Saroj Kumar have dedicated their lives to service, community empowerment, and sustainable development. After a distinguished military career, Col. Kumar returned to Hazaribagh with a commitment to “give back to society,” using his experience, skills, and resources to uplift local communities.

Their work today spans sustainable agriculture at Prakriti Biotech Farm, accessible community eye care through the Rohini Devi Rotary Eye Hospital, women’s vocational empowerment, and innovative environmental engineering solutions including the Prakriti Jal Urja Pump.

Together, they continue to create opportunities, restore livelihoods, and improve quality of life across Hazaribagh and surrounding regions.

Our Mission

To serve with compassion, strengthen local communities, and promote sustainable development through accessible healthcare, innovative farming, women’s economic empowerment, and environmentally responsible engineering.

Prakriti Biotech Farm

A model of organic farming inspired by traditional knowledge and modern sustainability practices. The farm produces organic crops, supports local livelihoods, and serves as a learning hub for environmentally conscious agriculture.

Rohini Devi Rotary Eye Hospital

A community-focused hospital offering cataract surgeries, screenings, and preventive care. Dedicated teams ensure that quality eye care is accessible to all, regardless of income.

Rotary Community Work

Through local and international partnerships, Rotary initiatives support education, health camps, disaster relief, vocational training, and programs designed to uplift underserved families.
