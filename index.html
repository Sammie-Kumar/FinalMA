Welcome to the Rohini Devi Rotary

The Rohini Devi Rotary is dedicated to fostering community development and humanitarian service. Through education, healthcare, and sustainability projects, we aim to empower lives and create lasting change.

Explore our initiatives — from the Eye Hospital’s life-changing vision programs to our sustainable farming projects that support local communities and promote environmental balance.

Our Mission

Our mission is to serve with compassion and commitment. We strive to improve quality of life through medical outreach, educational programs, and agricultural innovation — ensuring a brighter, more sustainable future for all.

Get Involved

Join us in our efforts to bring positive change. Whether by volunteering, donating, or spreading awareness, your involvement can make a meaningful difference in our community.
