Rohini Devi Rotary Organization

Rohini Devi Rotary is committed to service above self. Founded by Col. Binay Kumar (Retd), it focuses on empowering communities through education, healthcare, agriculture, and social development initiatives. Our members strive to make a meaningful difference locally and globally.

Our Core Initiatives

  • Education Programs: Scholarships, school infrastructure, and literacy campaigns.
  • Health & Wellness: Eye camps, vaccination drives, and awareness programs.
  • Agriculture Support: Farms, training, and resources for local farmers.
  • Community Development: Skill-building, clean water access, and empowerment projects.
  • Global Partnerships: Collaboration with Rotary International and other organizations.

Annual Eye Camp

Over 1,000 patients screened and treated each year, helping prevent blindness in underserved areas.

Farm Training Program

Local farmers trained in sustainable agriculture practices, improving food security and income.

Community Workshops

Workshops on hygiene, health, and education reach hundreds of community members annually.
